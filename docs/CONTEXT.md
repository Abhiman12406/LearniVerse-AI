# Adaptive Virtual Classroom

An agentic 3D cybernetic learning environment where the physical architecture, access barriers, and apparatuses dynamically adapt in response to learner mastery and prerequisite constraints.

## Actor & Embodiment

**Learner**:
The authoritative individual whose knowledge profile, mastery state, and learning history are maintained and evaluated.
_Avoid_: Student, user, client, player

**Avatar**:
The 3D embodied figure representing the learner's spatial presence and movement inside the virtual classroom.
_Avoid_: Character, player model, pawn

## Spatial Architecture & Navigation

**Atrium**:
The central hexagonal cybernetic hall connecting all wings and housing the central dais.
_Avoid_: Hub, lobby, central room, main hall

**Wing**:
A dedicated radial architectural corridor extending from the atrium into a specific subject domain.
_Avoid_: Zone, room, chamber, sector, branch

**Station**:
A specialized location within a wing equipped with learning consoles and apparatuses for hands-on exercises.
_Avoid_: Terminal, kiosk, desk, lab

**Central Dais**:
The elevated circular platform in the atrium that anchors the AI mentor and the holographic knowledge graph.
_Avoid_: Stage, podium, altar, platform

**Conduit**:
An emissive floor track that pulses with light to dynamically guide a learner toward a recommended station.
_Avoid_: Wire, path, trail, waypoint, track

## Physical & Interaction Machinery

**Apparatus**:
The physical in-world 3D kinetic machinery that visually and mechanically demonstrates a data structure's operations.
_Avoid_: Machine, widget, 3D object, simulation

**Console**:
The interactive interface at a station through which a learner views challenges, enters solutions, and receives feedback.
_Avoid_: Screen, panel, terminal, popup

## Pedagogical Progression & Mastery

**Challenge**:
An atomic, assessable learning exercise that tests a specific concept skill and yields a correctness evaluation.
_Avoid_: Question, problem, test item, quiz

**Mission**:
A structured learning objective assigned to a learner, composed of one or more sequential challenges.
_Avoid_: Task, quest, assignment, module

**Mastery**:
The probabilistic estimate of a learner's true understanding of a specific concept.
_Avoid_: Score, grade, progress, level

**Prerequisite Gap**:
A state where a learner's mastery in a foundational concept is below the threshold required to access an advanced wing.
_Avoid_: Deficiency, blocker, failure, deficit

**Knowledge Graph**:
The structured directed graph representing pedagogical concepts and their strict prerequisite relationships.
_Avoid_: Skill tree, concept map, curriculum graph

**Learner Evaluation Pipeline**:
The authoritative mathematical and diagnostic engine that ingests challenge interactions, scales evidence against technical validity, updates Bayesian Knowledge Tracing and Item Response Theory models, dynamically verifies prerequisite barriers, and triggers multi-agent deliberation.
_Avoid_: Scorer, grader, test checker, interaction processor

## Environmental Access Control

**Prerequisite Barrier**:
An energized holographic forcefield that seals a wing entrance whenever the learner has an unresolved prerequisite gap.
_Avoid_: Door, gate, wall, laser fence, forcefield

**Diagnostic Plaque**:
A floating holographic plaque projected at a sealed barrier displaying missing prerequisite concepts and required mastery thresholds.
_Avoid_: Sign, billboard, notice, warning plaque

**Barrier Dissolve**:
The environmental transformation event where a prerequisite barrier dematerializes when prerequisite mastery criteria are satisfied.
_Avoid_: Gate unlock, door open, deactivation

## Mentorship & Telemetry

**AI Mentor**:
An autonomous conversational guide situated on the central dais that provides contextual explanations and recommendations.
_Avoid_: Bot, NPC, tutor, assistant

**Telemetry Drawer**:
An inspectable real-time instrumentation panel displaying multi-agent decisions, reasoning traces, and mastery metrics.
_Avoid_: Debug panel, admin panel, log viewer, inspector
